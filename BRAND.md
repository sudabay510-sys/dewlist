# Dewlist — Brand identity

**Slug:** `dewlist` · **Tagline:** A simple AM/PM skincare habit plan — general education, not medical advice.
**Status:** Live. Payments processed by Gumroad (Monthly or Quarterly).

## Positioning
Adults who feel lost in the product aisle and want a simple, consistent routine — with clear limits.

**Promise (honest):** General education only. Not a diagnosis or treatment. No skin-results promises.

## Personas
- **Routine beginner:** You own a cleanser. Maybe. You want to start simple.
- **Product collector:** A shelf full of bottles and no idea what order they go in.
- **Sensitive-skin cautious:** You want gentle, slow, patch-tested steps.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Overwhelm** — pain: Too many products and opinions. → plan: A short AM/PM order matched to your time.
2. **Try** — pain: New products sting or break you out. → plan: One-at-a-time patch-test habit.
3. **Stick with it** — pain: Routines fade after a week. → plan: A 4-week habit tracker.
4. **Know when to ask** — pain: Unsure if something needs a doctor. → plan: Clear ‘see a dermatologist’ pointers.

## Voice & tone
Gentle, clear, science-respecting. Like a friend who reads labels — and tells you when to see a doctor.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing
- **Monthly** — $12 / month. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
- **Quarterly** — $29 every 3 months. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.

## Safety / compliance line
Dewlist doesn’t diagnose or treat skin conditions. For acne that hurts or scars, rashes, changing moles, or reactions, see a board-certified dermatologist or your doctor.

## Do
- Say ‘general education’ and ‘see a dermatologist’ plainly.
- Describe product types, never specific brands or prescription ingredients.
- Show Monthly and Quarterly prices, plus the renewal sentence, before checkout.
- Save the customer’s email before sending them to Gumroad.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Diagnose, name, or claim to treat conditions (acne, eczema, rosacea, etc.).
- Show before/after photos or promise visible results.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
